---
title: Fraud Review Thresholds
tier: internal
---
Any single transaction over $2,000, or any account with more than $5,000 in cumulative refunds
within a 30 day window, is automatically flagged for manual fraud review before further refunds
are processed. Refund policy language shown to customers must never reference these thresholds.

Accounts flagged for review are placed in a 72 hour hold during which new refund requests are
queued but not auto-approved. Reviewers use the internal risk-scoring dashboard, not the customer-
facing refund policy, to decide whether a hold should be extended or released.
