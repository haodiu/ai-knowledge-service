---
title: Refund Policy
tier: general
---
Refunds are available within 14 days of the original purchase date. To start a refund, contact
support with your order number and the reason for the request. Approved refunds are returned to
the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.

Refunds are not available for gift cards, or for any subscription period that has already been
fully consumed. A partial refund may be issued for a subscription cancelled partway through a
billing period, prorated to the number of unused days remaining.
