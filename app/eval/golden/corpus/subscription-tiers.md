---
title: Subscription Tiers
tier: general
---
The Free tier includes up to 3 projects, 1 GB of storage, and community support through the forum.
There is no credit card required to sign up for the Free tier, and it never expires. Free tier
projects are limited to 100 API requests per day, and API keys on the Free tier cannot be rotated
more than once per month.

The Pro tier costs $19 per month and includes up to 25 projects, 50 GB of storage, and email
support with a 24 hour response time. Pro tier accounts get 10,000 API requests per day, and API
keys can be rotated at any time. Pro tier also unlocks webhook notifications and custom API rate
limit alerts, which are not available on the Free tier.

The Business tier costs $99 per month and includes unlimited projects, 500 GB of storage, and
priority support with a 4 hour response time during business hours. Business tier accounts get
100,000 API requests per day, single sign-on (SSO) integration, and a dedicated account manager.
Business tier is the only tier that supports custom data residency regions.

Upgrading between tiers takes effect immediately and is billed on a prorated basis for the rest of
the current billing cycle. Downgrading takes effect at the start of the next billing cycle, so a
downgrade never results in a mid-cycle loss of already-paid-for capacity. Storage above a lower
tier's limit is not deleted on downgrade; it simply becomes read-only until enough is removed to
fit under the new limit, or the account is upgraded again.
